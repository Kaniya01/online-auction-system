Privacy Policy

This draft explains the data the current application handles. It does not assert certification or legal compliance.

Information used

The application stores account identifiers and profile details supplied during registration, auction and product information, bids, watchlist entries, recently viewed auctions, reports, and in-app notification history.

Use and visibility

Account and activity data support sign-in, auction operation, security, and relevant notifications. Watchlists, notifications, and recent views are scoped to their owner. Auction reports are restricted to authorized staff. Public auction pages display only information needed to operate listings and bidding.

Email and security

When configured, email is used for password reset and important auction events. Email delivery can fail; in-app notices remain the primary application record. Users should protect passwords and report suspected account misuse.

Retention and requests

Auction history and related bids may be retained to preserve marketplace records. Contact the platform owner for questions or requests about account information; procedures and retention periods require final owner review.
