Terms and Conditions

These draft terms describe intended platform behavior and are not legal advice or a claim of regulatory compliance.

Accounts and responsibilities

Users must provide accurate account details, keep credentials secure, and use only their own account. The same account may switch between Buyer and Seller modes; mode does not create separate accounts.

Buyer and seller obligations

Buyers should review product details and auction status before bidding. Sellers must describe products honestly, own or be authorized to list them, and honor the auction result subject to applicable law and any separately agreed transaction.

Bidding, closing and payment

Bids must exceed the current highest bid and are recorded in auction history. The highest valid bid when an auction closes is shown as the winner. Eligible winning bidders may be offered Razorpay Checkout. A payment is treated as paid only after server-side verification confirms capture. Test Mode transactions do not move real funds; platform records are not a receipt for a real-world transfer.

Prohibited activity and reporting

Fraud, manipulation, harassment, unlawful listings, and misleading descriptions are prohibited. Use the Report this listing action to alert authorized staff to suspicious content.

Platform limitations

Auctions may be cancelled by their seller while eligible. The platform provides listing, bidding, and payment-status records, but does not guarantee product condition, delivery, settlement, or dispute resolution.
