Auction Rules

Listing eligibility

Sellers may create an auction for an unarchived product they own. Product details should be accurate. Archived products cannot be used for new auctions.

Bidding

Only signed-in users other than the seller may bid. A bid must be greater than the displayed highest bid, be a valid amount to two decimal places, and arrive while the auction is active. Submitted bids are retained in history and cannot be withdrawn in the application.

Closing and winners

At the scheduled end, the highest recorded bid is identified as the winning bid. An auction with no bids has no winner. Cancellation is separate from completion, preserves bids, and prevents further bidding.

Suspicious listings

Users may submit one report per auction. Reports are reviewed by authorized staff; submitting a report does not guarantee removal or a particular outcome.

Payment limitation

AuctionHub has no implemented payment gateway. Winning bid values are not confirmed sales proceeds, payment requests, or proof that a transaction has completed.
